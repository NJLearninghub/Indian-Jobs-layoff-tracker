Disclaimer — please read before using these figures
  • This section compiles publicly reported figures for information and research only. It is not an official government publication and is not affiliated with any ministry, commission or recruitment board.
  • Vacancy and recruitment numbers are often provisional and are revised after publication. Several figures here are relayed through news or coaching websites rather than taken directly from the official notice.
  • Where any figure differs from the official source — the notification, gazette, Parliament reply or the recruiting body's website (e.g. ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, rrbcdg.gov.in, ibps.in) — the official source is correct and prevails.
  • These figures must not be relied on for applications, eligibility, seat counts, legal claims, RTI disputes or any other formal purpose. Always check the latest official notification.
  • Every row links to the source it was taken from. To report an error, include the official document and we will correct it.
